There are previous year JNTUH question papers. We have 5-6 subjects per semester and there are 2 semesters per year. JNTUH question papers follow specific patterns in terms of unit-wise weightage, repeated topics, question types e.t.c. 

The system produces a consumer technology which generates the next question paper based on the current knowledge base. The paper is generated by a set of rubrics. The rubrics are defined based on the previous year question papers.
